Please follow below sequences berthing allotment.

1)  Government Priority – me 2 vessels ko berth milega (Geared vessel
    can berth from CJ 1 to CJ 8 and ungeared vessel can berth from CJ 7
    to CJ8). Government priority Seniority basis lagna hai. Government
    priority full karneke baad. Agar koi vessel nai hai Government
    priority pe to berth allotment next priority pe jayega jo hai
    coastal Priority

2)  Coastal Priority - me 1 vessels ko berth milega. Is priority me
    berthing seniority ke hisab se allotment hota hai. Agar koi vessel
    nai hai coastal priority me to next allotment High productivity
    priority pe jayega.

3)  High productivity - me 2 berth allot kiye jayenge. HP me allotment
    ke liye jo vessels ka parcel size 40000 mts ya us se jyada hai wo hi
    HP ke liye qualified hai. Jo vessel ready hai unke bich me bid ki
    jati hai. Bid 40000 mts se start hota hai or max 50000 mts tak ja
    sakte hai. HP me jo vessel max bid karta hai usko allotment milta
    hai. Agar sare vessel 50000 mts tak jate hai ya same bid lagate hai
    to usme berting seniority ko milta hai. HP allotment full karne ke
    baad ya agar koi vessel nai hai HP priority me to berth allotment
    next priority pe jeyaga to hai Saagar Priority ko.

4)  SAAGAR PRIORITY – me 2 vessels ko berth milega. 1 berth jayega Break
    bulk and 1 berth jayega Bulk cargo ko. Iska allotment seniority ke
    base pe hota hai. At one time 1 vessel in each category me rahega.
    Agar koi category me saagar priority me vessel nai hai to allotment
    dusre category me lag sakta hai. Example 1 vessel break bulk me hai
    or bulk me koi nai hai to 2 vessel bhi break bulk ka lag sakta hai.
    Saagar priority fullfill karne ke baad agar Government or Coastal or
    HP Priority me koi slot khali hai to uski jagah pe 1^(st) Saagar
    Priority (Project) vessel ko berth milega.

5)  Saagar Priority (Project) – me 1 vessel hi berth ho sakta hai. Jo
    Project vessel Saagar me declared hai us ko Saagar me allotment hoga
    agar Government ya coastal ya HP me se kisi me berth khali hai to hi
    milega. Agar is me bhi koi vessel nai hai to next allotment
    30k/25k/10k ko jayega.

6)  30K/25K/10K – ye priority sirf tab open hoga or vessels lagega jab
    Government priority me 1 berth khali hai ya fir Coastal priority me
    berth khali hai. Jo priority me khali hoga uske against ye priority
    me vessel lagega. Is priority me jo vessels ready hai 30000 mts
    karne ko usko senirity ke hisab se milega. Agar koi vessel 30000 mts
    me nai hai to 25000 karne ko jo vessels ready honge unko seniority
    ke hisabse milega or 25000 mts karne ke liy koi nai hai to 10000 mts
    karne ko jo vessel sready honge unko seniority ke hisabse milega.
    Agar koi nai hai to next priority 24 hrs priority ko berth milega.

7)  24 hrs Priority – me 1 vessel ko berth milega. Jo vessel ready hai
    24 hrs me compelte kar ke sail hone ke liye usko berth milega
    seniority ke basis pe. Agar koi vessel 24 hrs me compelte kar ke
    sail hone wale nai hai to uski jagh pe agar koi vessel 30k/25k/10k
    mer ready hai to usko berth milega. Is priority me jo vessels ready
    hai 30000 mts karne ko usko senirity ke hisab se milega. Agar koi
    vessel 30000 mts me nai hai to 25000 karne ko jo vessels ready honge
    unko seniority ke hisabse milega or 25000 mts karne ke liy koi nai
    hai to 10000 mts karne ko jo vessel sready honge unko seniority ke
    hisabse milega. Ye priority fullfill karne ke baad ya Agar koi nai
    hai to next priority 8000/6500 k priority ko berth milega.

8)  8000/6500 k Prioirity – me 1 vessel ko berth milega. Jo vessel ready
    hai 8000 ya 6500 mts per day karne ke hisabse wo vessles seniority
    ke hisab se berth allotment hoga. Is priority ko fullfill karne ke
    baad ya agar koi vessel nai hai to allotment next priority
    Steel/Project Priority ko milega.

9)  Steel/Project Priority – me 1 vessel ko berth milega. Jo vessel
    Steel ya project cargo ke saath ready hai usko seniority ke hisabse
    berth milega. Is priority ko fullfill karne ke baad ya agar koi
    vessel nai hai to allotment next priority Bitumen Priority ko
    milega.

10) Bitumen Priority – me 1 vessel ko berth milega jo vessel ka cargo
    Bitumen hai. Agar Bitumen priority me koi vessel nai hai to uska
    berth 6500 mts me jo break bulk vessel ready hai unko seniority ke
    hisabse milega. Is priority ko fullfill karne ke baad ya agar koi
    vessel nai hai to allotment next allotment general group ko milega.

11) General – me 3 vessels ko berth milega. Most 3 senior vessel ko
    berth milega respectively. Agar iske baad koi berth khali hai to wo
    berth jayega Sr. No. 7 Priority me. Is me jo vessel norms + 15%
    handle karne ko ready hai usko seniority ke hisabse berth milega.

Other rules:

Port ki daily berthing meeting 1100 hrs par hoti hai, jisme 1800 hrs
same day se lekar next day 1800 hrs tak ke vessel movements decide kiye
jaate hain. Meeting mein wahi vessels eligible hoti hain jo 1100 hrs tak
Port par arrive ho chuki hoti hain aur jinhe 1100 hrs tak ready declare
kar diya gaya hota hai. Iske alawa, jo vessels expected mein hoti hain,
unko bhi meeting mein consider kiya jaata hai, lekin unka arrival same
day 1800 hrs se next day 1800 hrs ke movement period ke andar aane wale
first High Water se pehle ya usi samay hona chahiye.

For example, agar aaj ki date 27.11.2026 hai, toh 27.11.2026 ke 1800 hrs
se 28.11.2026 ke 1800 hrs tak ke movement period mein agar first High
Water 2100 hrs par hai, toh jo vessels 2100 hrs tak ya usse pehle
expected hain, wahi vessels 27.11.2026 ki meeting mein berth
consideration ke liye eligible hongi. Lekin jo vessels 2100 hrs ke baad
expected hain, woh 27.11.2026 ki meeting mein eligible nahi hongi aur
unhe next day's berthing meeting mein consideration ke liye liya jayega.

Normally jo berth khali ho raha hai jis group me us group ko hi us jagah
pe berth milega. Agar jo berth khali ho raha hai wo group me next vessel
ke liye suitable berth nai hai to usko pehle dusri jo berth khali ho
rahi hai vaha pe berth milega. Agar kisiko shift karne ki jarurat padi
to usko shift karke first group ko berth diya jayega.
