No. Traffic /Shipping/587 /Berthingpolicy/2026/523¢ Dated:21.11.2026

CIRCULAR
In partial modification of the previously circulated Berthing Policy and its Corrigendum, the Competent
Authority has approved the following modifications to the Berthing Policy:
(i) In the absence of suitable vessels under the respective Government Priority and Coastal Priority
categories, berth allotment shall proceed sequentially in accordance with the prescribed order of
priority, up to and including Sr. No. 3(B).
(ii) Productivity Norms — Timber Logs — Pine Logs & Hardwood:
The existing base productivity norms for Timber Logs — Pine Logs at Sr. 12 under Break Bulk Cargo in
Appendix-02A are hereby revised to a uniform base productivity norm of 4,000 MTPD, irrespective of
parcel size and bulk density. The existing base productivity norms for Timber Logs — Hardwood at Sr.
13 is hereby revised to a base productivity norm of 5,500 MTPD All other applicable productivity and
priority eligibility provisions remain unchanged.
(iii) Bitumen Priority (Sr. No. 5):
In the absence of a suitable vessel under Bitumen Priority, the berth earmarked under this category
shall be operated under the 6,500 MTPD Priority category (Sr. No. 3(E)), instead of the existing
provision of operating the berth under the General Group. Allotment shall be subject to the applicable
productivity eligibility criteria, readiness, seniority and other conditions prescribed under the Berthing
Policy.
All other terms and conditions contained in the Comprehensive Berthing Policy dated 24.03.2025,
Circular dated 21.05.2026 and Corrigendum dated 09.07.2026 shall continue to remain in force and
shall be unaffected by this Circular.
The aforementioned modifications shall come into effect from the proceedings of the Berthing
Meeting to be held on 23/11/2026 at 1100 hrs and shall remain valid until further orders.
This has the approval of the Competent Authority. ye
