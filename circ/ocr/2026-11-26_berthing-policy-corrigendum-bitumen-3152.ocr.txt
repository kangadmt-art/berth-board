CORRIGENDUM

Ref: Circular No. Traffic/Shipping/587/Berthingpolicy/2026/5737 dated 21.11.2026.

In partial modification of Clause (iii) - Bitumen Priority (Sr. No. 5) of the Circular cited above, the
following clarification is hereby issued:

(iii) Bitumen Priority (Sr. No. 5):
In the absence of a suitable vessel under Bitumen Priority, the berth earmarked under this category shall
be operated under the 6,500 MTPD Priority category (Sr. No. 3(E)) for Break Bulk Cargo only.

All other terms and conditions contained in the Comprehensive Berthing Policy dated 24.03.2025,
Circular dated 21.05.2026, Corrigendum dated 09.07.2026 and Circular dated 21.11.2026 shall continue
to remain in force and shall be unaffected by this Corrigendum.

This has the approval of the Competent Authority.
