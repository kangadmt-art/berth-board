How to read this page

Tuna Tekra is a PPP terminal run by Adani inside Deendayal Port. Its berths are not in the DPA berthing-policy queue, so the board makes no projection here — the order of berthing is whatever the list's remarks say ("TT-03 followed by …"). The DPA list prints no discharge norm or ETC for TT vessels; "days alongside" is counted from the list's MADE FAST time. TT movements do not appear on the Harbour Master's cargo-jetty signals, so a berthing taken from a B'TODAY remark stays marked as not confirmed until a list prints it.
