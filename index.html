North end (CJ-1) left, south (CJ-16) right. Solid hull = at berth as per list/signal. Dotted hull = projected by the board. Click a hull to look it up above. Drawn to scale in metres from the Civil Engg "Cargo Berth Details" sheet (CJ-1–4 182.93 m, CJ-5–6 205.79, CJ-7 238.64, CJ-8 213.64, CJ-9 182.93, CJ-10 209.14, CJ-13–16 300 each); permissible draught from the Deputy Conservator's chart. Panel marks are not equal: ~22.9 m on CJ-1–6, ~34 m on CJ-7, ~21 m on CJ-8–10, and on CJ-13–16 the HM's marks 111–189 run ~15.4 m apart over the 1,200 m (the Civil sheet counts 20 m structural panels there, 131–145 / 146–159 / 160–174). Tidal benefit applies at CJ-6–10 only. Quay live load (Civil Engg sheet): 3.3 t/m² at CJ-1–5 and CJ-6 panels 42–45, 3.5 t/m² quay-side at CJ-7–9, 5 t/m² at CJ-10–16 and in the transit areas behind CJ-6–9 (IIT Mumbai report), which is why heavy stacking stays off the north berths. Expected vs actual · logic check
