Accuracy · berthing predictions

Mihir's measure (13 Nov): each day, from that morning's DPR and the berthing policy, the board says which berth a waiting vessel gets and when. That call is frozen and scored against the actual berthing (DPR made-fast time) — before the port's programme or signal for it existed. A programme-based call is not a prediction and is not counted. Hit = right berth and within 6 h · near = right berth, within 12 h · miss = wrong berth, > 12 h, or not predicted at all. Sailing times are not scored (a sailing two hours late is not important). Record starts with DPR 11.11.

Tuna · TT-1 to TT-4 (Adani PPP)

How to read this board

Tuna Tekra is a PPP terminal run by Adani inside Deendayal Port. Its berths are not in the DPA berthing-policy queue, so the board makes no projection here — the order of berthing is whatever the list's remarks say ("TT-03 followed by …"). The DPA list prints no discharge norm or ETC for TT vessels; "days alongside" is counted from the list's MADE FAST time. TT movements do not appear on the Harbour Master's cargo-jetty signals, so a berthing taken from a B'TODAY remark stays marked as not confirmed until a list prints it.

Oil-jetty movements · HM signals

How to read this board

Allotment pattern (berthing policy, liquid appendix): OJ-1 LPG carriers by inter-se seniority — and since 16.03.2026 every LPG vessel has priority berthing (MoPSW direction); OJ-1 to OJ-4 all compatible cargo by seniority (at most two edible-oil tankers at a time); OJ-5 IFFCO (ammonia / phosphoric acid) — a non-IFFCO vessel working there is shifted out at port convenience when an IFFCO tanker needs the jetty (circular 1646 dt 05.03.2026, valid to 31.03.2027); OJ-6 IOCL / BPCL (POL); OJ-7 the senior-most edible / vegetable-oil tanker. Norms printed in the list (MTPH): edible oil 275 / 415 / 525 / 650 and chemicals 225 / 360 / 475 / 550 by parcel size (≤ 5,250 / ≤ 10,500 / ≤ 21,000 / above — corrigendum 2394 dt 09.07.2026), export cargo 250, LPG 380 butane / 500 propane / 775 both. See the circulars library on the Cargo tab for the PDFs.
The board has no berthing rules for liquid cargo, so nothing here is projected: occupancy is the DPA list plus the Harbour Master's signals, and the queue is shown exactly as the list prints it — the letter after the rank is the list's own category (E edible oils such as CDSBO / CPO, C chemicals, O POL such as HSD / MS / VLSFO); "REQ OJ 2, 3, 4" is the agent's berth requirement from the list remarks. IFFCO's ammonia and phosphoric-acid vessels appear under OJ in the list. "Made fast" times derived from an "o/c" (on completion) signal are estimates until a list prints them. The jetty map is a schematic row in OJ order, not the geographic layout.
